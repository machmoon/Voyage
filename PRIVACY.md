# Voyage Privacy Notice

Effective September 7, 2026

Voyage is designed to work without an account. Focus-session and logbook data,
including routes, seats, intentions, completion status, replay samples, and
frozen weather, is stored on your device using SwiftData unless you remove the
app or clear its data.

With permission, Voyage uses your approximate device location to choose the
nearest supported home airport. That location is used on the device only: the
app stores the resulting airport code so it can start offline, and your
coordinates are never sent to any server. Local notifications and Focus status
are used only for the flight reminders you enable in Settings.

Map scenery is drawn with Apple's MapKit. Apple receives the technical
information needed to serve map content, such as requested coordinates, IP
address, and device and app metadata, and describes its practices in the
[Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Apple Maps
attribution remains visible in the airplane window and on the moving map.

Voyage requests current weather for the departure and arrival airports. Those
requests are handled by [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com), and they send the
airport's published coordinates — not your location. Voyage does not use Apple
WeatherKit. The frozen results are attached to the local flight replay so the
scene does not change later. Voyage does not use these requests to build an
advertising profile.

Voyage contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking
software of any kind.

You control scenery, notifications, Focus reminders, and home-airport selection
in Settings. You can delete Voyage's stored data by clearing the logbook where
offered or uninstalling the app.

Questions can be opened in the
[Voyage issue tracker](https://github.com/machmoon/Voyage/issues).
