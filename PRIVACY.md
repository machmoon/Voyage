# Voyage Privacy Notice

Effective September 8, 2026

Voyage is designed to work without an account. Voyage does not collect personal
data. Focus-session and logbook data, including routes, seats, intentions,
completion status, replay samples, and frozen weather, is stored on your device
using SwiftData unless you remove the app or clear its data. None of it is sent
to Voyage or to anyone else.

With permission, Voyage uses your approximate device location to choose the
nearest supported airport. The lookup happens on your device against a bundled
airport list. Voyage stores the resulting airport code so it can start offline;
it does not retain a location history and never sends your position anywhere.
Local notifications and Focus status are used only for the flight reminders you
enable in Settings.

When streamed scenery is enabled, the airplane window and the flight map draw
satellite and terrain imagery from Apple Maps through MapKit. Apple receives the
technical information needed to serve map content, such as requested
coordinates, IP address, and device and app metadata. Apple describes its
practices in the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
Apple's attribution stays visible wherever that imagery is shown. Voyage uses no
other mapping provider.

Voyage requests current weather for airports and a small number of route sample
points from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com), which is credited in Settings
under the [CC BY 4.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Those requests carry only the coordinates of the airport or route sample, which
come from Voyage's bundled route catalog and are never your own position. The
frozen results are attached to the local flight replay so the scene does not
change later. If a reading is unavailable, Voyage computes clear conditions on
device. Voyage does not use these requests to build an advertising profile.

Voyage contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs of any
kind.

You control streamed scenery, notifications, Focus reminders, and airport
selection in Settings. You can delete Voyage's stored data with Clear Logbook in
Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Questions can be opened in the
[Voyage issue tracker](https://github.com/machmoon/Voyage/issues).
