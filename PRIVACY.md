# Voyage Privacy Notice

Effective July 21, 2026

Voyage is designed to work without an account. Focus-session and logbook data,
including routes, seats, intentions, completion status, replay samples, and
frozen weather, is stored on your device using SwiftData unless you remove the
app or clear its data.

With permission, Voyage uses your approximate device location to choose the
nearest supported home airport. Voyage stores the resulting airport code so it
can start offline; it does not need to retain a location history. Local
notifications and Focus status are used only for the flight reminders you
enable in Settings.

When Real-World Twin is enabled, the app can request streamed maps from Google
Maps Platform or Apple Maps. Those providers receive the technical information
needed to serve map content, such as requested coordinates, IP address, and
device/app metadata. Google processes this information under the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Apple describes
its practices in the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
Provider attribution remains visible in the airplane window.

Voyage requests current weather for airports and a small number of route sample
points. Depending on availability, requests can be handled by Apple WeatherKit
or Open-Meteo. The frozen results are attached to the local flight replay so the
scene does not change later. Voyage does not use these requests to build an
advertising profile.

You control streamed scenery, notifications, Focus reminders, and home-airport
selection in Settings. You can delete Voyage's stored data by clearing the
logbook where offered or uninstalling the app.

Questions can be opened in the
[Voyage issue tracker](https://github.com/machmoon/Voyage/issues).
