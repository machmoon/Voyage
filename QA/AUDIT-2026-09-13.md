## Against FocusFlight (App Store, 4.9 from 9,300 ratings, Editors' Choice)

What they have that Voyage does not:

- **App blocking through Screen Time.** Voyage's listing promises not to block other apps; keep that, but it is the single most-cited reason people pay for FocusFlight.
- **Pure mode**: a view with only the remaining time, reached by a double tap. Cheap to add to `InFlightView` and matches Voyage's "the study screen stays calm" stance.
- **Random route** when the traveler does not care where they go.
- **Focus music** alongside white noise; reviewers complain their ocean and rain tracks are harsh, which is an opening for Voyage's generated cabin ambience.
- **Meal-break requests** on long sessions, which Voyage already answers with layovers and cabin service.

What Voyage has that they do not: real block times and carriers, seat choice with a real window side, the torn pass, layovers, passport stamps, trip replay, Live Activity, weather in the window, and no subscription (FocusFlight is $3.99 a month or $18.99 a year).

## How to reproduce the sweep

```bash
xcodebuild -project Voyage.xcodeproj -scheme Voyage \
  -destination 'platform=iOS Simulator,name=iPhone 17' test -only-testing:VoyageUITests
python3 - <<'EOF'   # contact sheets
from PIL import Image; import glob
fs = sorted(glob.glob('QA/e2e-ax-*.png')); ims = [Image.open(f) for f in fs]
w, h = ims[0].size; s = 0.3; sheet = Image.new('RGB', (int(w*s)*len(ims), int(h*s)))
for i, im in enumerate(ims): sheet.paste(im.resize((int(w*s), int(h*s))), (i*int(w*s), 0))
sheet.save('QA/review/ax.png')
EOF
```
